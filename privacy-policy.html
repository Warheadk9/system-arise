Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information THE AWAKENING ("the app," "we," "us") collects from players ("you," "hunters"), how it's used, and what choices you have. By creating an account and using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and a password (stored securely by Firebase Authentication, we never see your raw password).
  • Profile information: username, rank, gold, XP, inventory, and other in-game progress.
  • Messages: chat messages sent to other hunters, including any photos or files you choose to share.
  • Usage data: basic gameplay activity (battles fought, items purchased) needed to run the game.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your hunter account and game progress.
  • To let you chat and battle with other hunters.
  • To send you notifications about messages, challenges, and daily rewards (if enabled on your device).
  • To keep the game fair and secure, and to prevent cheating or abuse.

3. Who Can See Your Information

Your username, rank, and hunter title are visible to other hunters on the site, since this is a multiplayer game. Your email address is never shown to other players. We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored using Google Firebase, a third-party cloud service. Firebase has its own security and privacy practices, which you can read about at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Your Choices

  • You can delete your account at any time from your profile settings.
  • You can turn off notifications in your device settings at any time.
  • You can contact us to request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it.

6. Children's Privacy

THE AWAKENING is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under this age.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
